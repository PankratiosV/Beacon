Beacon: live laser status for LightBurn

Beacon watches LightBurn and shows what your laser is doing (Running, Paused, Framing, Ready or Disconnected) in a small window or floating pill, on a USB or Wi-Fi stack light, and on your phone. It also times every job and keeps statistics.

This installer puts Beacon in your Applications folder. Everything it needs is included, so there's nothing else to download.

  • Works with any LightBurn version (reads the screen), or with LightBurn 2.2+ through its API, even while LightBurn is minimised.
  • Needs macOS 12.3 or newer, on an Apple Silicon or Intel Mac.

Safety: Beacon is a convenience, not a safety device. Never leave a running laser unattended.
